Growth Spurt Guide & Signs

Infant growth spurts typically last 2 to 4 days. Babies burn enormous energy rapidly growing bones, muscles, and brain pathways!

Cluster Feeds Hunger surges every 1–2 hours
Sleep Changes Extra sleepy OR waking often
Fussiness Wants more skin-to-skin & hugs
Common Spurt Schedule (Weeks):
Week 1–2
7 to 10 Days: First Growth Spurt

Regains birth weight, establishes mother's breastmilk supply with non-stop latching.

Week 2–3
2 to 3 Weeks: Rapid Hunger Surge

Major physical leap. Frequent night wakings and higher feeding amounts.

Week 6–8
6 Weeks: The "Awakening" Peak

Often the most intense spurt. Baby becomes highly alert, smiles socially, but can be fussy at dusk (the "witching hour").

Week 12
3 Months: Physical & Visual Leap

Lengthens in inches, clothing outgrown overnight, laughs and coos constantly.

Month 6
6 Months: Doubling Birth Weight

Major motor activity (rolling, sitting). Often coincides with readiness for solid foods.

Month 9
9 Months: Crawling & Brain Expansion

High mobility, object permanence, exploration, and teething discomfort.
